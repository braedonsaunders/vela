libmpv-2.dll in this folder
===========================

Vela for Windows plays video through libmpv-2.dll, a separately linked, replaceable library.
Replace the file with any compatible libmpv build to use your own; Vela loads it by name.

Build: the LGPL variant of mpv-winbuild-cmake (https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake)
as published by https://github.com/zhongfly/mpv-winbuild, release 2026-11-22-c2a42f414f,
file mpv-dev-lgpl-x86_64-20261122-git-c2a42f414f.7z,
SHA-256 47540e48af9918f044688cb178d9e89d348f4c11868ad67bced3bc074abff8a9.

- mpv commit c2a42f414fcb5f8a21c9cd9a311d65932a5ef0cc, configured with -Dgpl=false:
  LGPL-2.1-or-later (LGPL-2.1.txt).
- FFmpeg git 329157f58, configured without --enable-gpl and without --enable-nonfree, with
  --enable-version3: LGPL-3.0-or-later (LGPL-3.0.txt, which incorporates GPL-3.0.txt).
- Statically linked into the same DLL: libplacebo (LGPL-2.1-or-later), libass (ISC),
  FreeType (FTL), HarfBuzz (MIT), FriBidi (LGPL-2.1-or-later), dav1d (BSD-2-Clause),
  libbluray (LGPL-2.1-or-later), lcms2 (MIT), zlib and that build's other non-GPL
  dependencies.

Source: the complete corresponding source for this libmpv-2.dll is available on request for
three years from the date of the Vela release that shipped it: open an issue at
https://github.com/braedonsaunders/vela/issues.

Dolby, Dolby Atmos, Dolby TrueHD and Dolby Vision are trademarks of Dolby Laboratories; DTS,
DTS-HD and DTS:X are trademarks of DTS, Inc. Vela does not encode Dolby or DTS audio. Bitstream
passthrough sends the film's own audio, unchanged, to the receiver, which holds the decoding
licence. When a receiver cannot take a bitstream, FFmpeg's own decoders turn it into PCM.
FFmpeg's TrueHD decoder gives the 7.1 bed without Atmos objects, and its DTS decoder gives the
lossless DTS-HD Master Audio channels without DTS:X objects. Dolby Vision is shown by
libplacebo applying the stream's own RPU metadata. It is converted to HDR10 or SDR for the
display, and no Dolby Vision signal is sent.
