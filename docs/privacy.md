# Vela privacy policy

Vela is a player for media servers you choose: Ether, Jellyfin, Emby or Plex. It has no account
of its own and no Vela-operated service.

**What Vela collects.** Nothing. Vela has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting
services, and the developer receives no data from the app.

**What stays on your device.** The servers you add, their sign-in sessions (in the system
keychain), your profile choice and playback and display preferences. For a Plex server linked
directly, the sign-in kept beside it includes your Plex account's, used only to switch Plex Home
profiles. Removing a server, signing out of it or removing the app removes them.

**What goes to your servers.** Vela talks only to the servers you add, and to the addresses those
servers give it for artwork and media. Those servers receive what any of their clients sends: your
sign-in, what you browse and play, and your playback progress. Their own privacy policies apply.
Signing in with Plex also goes through plex.tv, which links your Plex account and lists your
servers, under Plex's privacy policy.

**Local network.** On Apple devices Vela asks for local network access so it can reach servers on
your home network. It does not scan or report other devices.

**Children.** Vela is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
