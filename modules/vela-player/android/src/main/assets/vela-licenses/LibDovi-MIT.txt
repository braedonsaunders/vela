libvela_dovi.so statically links libdovi, the dolby_vision crate of dovi_tool 3.4.0
(https://github.com/quietvoid/dovi_tool, tag libdovi-3.4.0), used to convert Dolby Vision
Profile 7 RPU metadata to Profile 8.1. Built from pinned source by
modules/vela-player/android/dovi/build.sh.
