Vela · identity · round one

Five directions for the mark

Each is drawn from scratch as vector geometry (letterforms included, except Italic, which uses the bundled OFL face as outlines). Every direction is shown animated, as a symbol, wordmark and lockup on dark and light, at true favicon size and as an app icon. Symbols and wordmarks can be mixed between directions. Motion uses only what Vela's toolkit can play (opacity, transforms, stroke drawing, masks) and stays under two seconds; reduced motion shows the finished mark.

01

Sail V

Editorial · Didone

The V of Vela drawn as a high-contrast Didone letter whose heavy stroke bellies out like a sail in the wind, its hairline the stay that holds it. A single star rises between them. Paired with custom Didone capitals, flat hairline serifs and no curves at all, because V, E, L and A need none.

Feels like
Criterion, A24 posters, a title card set in metal type.
Motion
The stay draws up from the keel, the sail fills from below, the star ignites, light passes across; the capitals settle in one by one.
02

Projector

Cinematic · hairline capitals

Two sails hang as a V, and between them a blade of light opens from a single point, the projector beam in a dark room. The wordmark is hand-built hairline capitals with a crossbarless Λ that answers the V, tracked wide like an opening title.

Feels like
Quiet, nocturnal, the moment the house lights go down.
Motion
The beam rises from the point, the sails part out of the dark, then the letters drift outward from the centre into their wide tracking.
03

Horizon

Iconic · geometric capitals

The name's two pointed letters, Λ over V, meet as a four-pointed star split by a line of light: a star on the horizon, a sail on the sea. The split keeps it from being a generic sparkle. Sturdy geometric capitals with the same Λ carry it at any size.

Feels like
Confident and simple; reads at 16 px and on a TV across the room.
Motion
A horizon line draws outward, the star rises out of it and its reflection sinks below, the letters surface from the baseline.
04

Duet

Modern · monoline lowercase

A V made of two sails, one in shadow and one catching the light, overlapping at the keel. Warm and approachable, a strong app icon. The wordmark is a custom monoline lowercase whose stroke matches the mark, so the whole lockup can be drawn with one pen of light.

Feels like
Friendly, product-like, at home on a phone home screen.
Motion
The two sails glide in and meet at the keel, then each letter is drawn as a single stroke.
05

Italic

Evolution · Instrument Serif

The closest to today's mark, made real: Instrument Serif Italic (SIL OFL, already bundled) set as outlines and tightened, with the star moved to sit like a guide star off the v. Keeps continuity with the cinematic skin's display face.

Feels like
Literary, warm, a little romantic.
Motion
Light sweeps left to right, revealing the v and the word, and the star twinkles on last.
