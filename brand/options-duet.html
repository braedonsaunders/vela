Vela · identity · round two · Duet

Duet, as searchlights

The two sails of Duet become two searchlights shining up from the ground, the way lamps light the sky outside a premiere. Each variation keeps Duet's pair of warm white and champagne (shadow and light) and its custom monoline lowercase. They differ in how many lamps there are, how hard the light is, how the beams move and where the star sits. Every animation is under two seconds and then holds still, and reduced motion shows the finished mark. At 16 px each one falls back to a solid two-tone V.

01

Premiere

One lamp · hard beams · cross and settle

Duet's two sails become two searchlights fired from a single lamp, one in warm white and one in champagne, still the shadow-and-light pair. Hard edges keep a crisp V from 16 px to the big screen. On the way up the beams cross the sky and catch a star.

Motion
The lamp strikes, both beams rise crossed, sweep through each other (catching the star as they pass) and settle into the V. Then the letters are drawn.
02

Volumetric

One lamp · soft light · rising straight

The most atmospheric version: real light that is brightest at the lamp and dissolves into haze, with a bloom around the beams. For the app icon and favicon it switches to a solid small cut with the same silhouette, because soft light turns to mush at 16 px.

Motion
The lamp flickers on, the beams rise straight up out of the ground and the haze blooms around them. No sweep, just light arriving.
03

Two lamps

Two lamps · hard beams · crossing at the ground

Two searchlights stand a step apart on a ground line, each aimed across the other, so the beams cross just above the ground and open into the V. It is the most premiere-like: a pair of lamps outside a theatre. The small crossing at the base gives the mark a distinctive foot.

Motion
The ground line draws, two lamps strike, the beams rise upright and parallel, then tilt across each other into place.
04

Guide star

One lamp · lit falloff · search and find

The beams light the night together, sweep across it as if searching, then part to frame the star they have found, Vela's star above the V. The beams stay hard-edged but fade slightly towards the sky so they read as light, not paper.

Motion
The lamp strikes, both beams lean left and sweep together across the sky, then part into the V, and the star ignites between them.
