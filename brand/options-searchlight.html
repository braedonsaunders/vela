Vela · identity · round three · searchlights

Duet, with the sweep

The searchlight wave: slow, graceful swings that cross and uncross out of phase, each smaller than the last, easing into the resting pose and then holding still (about three seconds). Two families. In A the beams are the V. In B the mark stays a solid Duet V and wordmark, and the light is atmosphere behind it. Each stage has Replay, a Loop toggle to watch the wave repeatedly, and an optional idle sway meant only for the splash screen, never for icons. With reduced motion the finished mark shows straight away.

Family A

The beams are the V

The searchlights themselves draw the letter. Strongest single idea; the motion and the mark are one.

A1

Crossfire

A · beams are the V · one lamp, mirrored crossing

The classic premiere rhythm: two searchlights from one lamp cross, uncross, cross again, each pass smaller, and come to rest as Duet's two-tone V. The star above the V is caught as the beams pass through it. Hard edges keep the V crisp from 16 px up.

A2

Twin lamps

A · beams are the V · two lamps, crossing at the ground

Two lamps a step apart on the ground, each aimed across the other, as they stand outside a theatre. The beams wave in opposite directions, opening wide and folding back across each other, then settle into the V with its small crossed foot.

A3

Search

A · beams are the V · one lamp, sweeping together

Instead of mirroring, the two beams sweep the sky together, the second a beat behind the first, like operators searching for something. They swing wide, swing back, find the star and open into the V beneath it. Light falls off slightly towards the sky so the beams read as light, not paper.

Family B

The light is atmosphere

The V and the name stay a clean, solid Duet mark. Searchlights rise from behind or around it and wave across the sky. The light belongs to the splash and large moments, and icons stay simple.

B1

Behind the V

B · light behind a solid mark · lamps behind the symbol

The V stays Duet's clean two-tone letter. Two searchlights stand behind it and wave across the sky, crossing above it, then rest opened a little wider than the V so the mark seems to give off light. Icons and favicons are the plain V; the light belongs to the splash and large lockups.

B2

Marquee

B · light above the wordmark · lamps flanking the name

The wordmark is the marquee. Two lamps stand at either end of the name and wave their beams across the sky above it, crossing and uncrossing, until they come to rest crossed high overhead, the picture of a premiere. The name and V stay solid and legible throughout.

B3

Night badge

B · light inside an emblem · app-icon first

An emblem built for the home screen: a night-sky tile where two searchlights wave behind the solid V and come to rest fanning out from behind it. On small sizes the tile keeps just the V, so it stays crisp; at icon size and above the light is part of the badge.
