Vela · identity · final round

The V is the light

The Night badge V at rest, with nothing behind it: the two arms are the two searchlights. The light side reaches the ground and the shadow side stops short against it, two-tone everywhere. Each beam is a motorised lamp that pivots rigidly from its base. The lamps power on with a short flicker, sweep at constant speed with crisp reversals, then drive into the V and stop dead. No bounce and no drift. The variants differ only in the choreography. Each stage shows the splash on a TV and on a phone, with Replay and Loop. With reduced motion the V is shown already locked.

01

Mirrored · two passes

Both motors sweep in mirror image, crossing at the centre

The two beams sweep in mirror image at a steady speed, crossing at the centre of each pass, and reverse crisply at the ends like a pair of motors on a timer. Two full passes, then they drive into the V and lock. A short flash of full power marks the stop.

Offset · two passes

Both motors sweep the same way, the second a beat behind

The beams sweep the same direction, the shadow side a beat behind the light side, like two lamps driven from one control desk. They track across the sky twice and lock into the V one after the other, a two-step click into place.

Mirrored · three quick passes

Faster motors, three passes, a harder lock

The same mirrored crossing as the first, driven faster: three brisk passes with snap reversals, then a hard stop into the V with the full-power flash. More energy, still mechanical; reads well on a TV across the room.
